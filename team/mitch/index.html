The Architect

Mitch Strickland founded Strickland Productions to build the apps he couldn't find anywhere else: tools that fit the way people actually watch, play and collect. That's why the studio calls him The Architect. He draws up the blueprint for every project: what it does, how it looks, how it feels to use, and what gets cut because it's in the way.

Maya handles the construction. The Architect walks the site, tests every room and sends back anything that isn't finished.

The vision

His rule is simple: software should get out of the way. If a job takes five taps, it should take one. If something can be filled in automatically, nobody should have to type it. Clean screens, no clutter, and nothing that makes you think harder than you need to.

Where the ideas come from

Every app starts with something The Architect does himself. A lifelong gamer, he wanted one place to track his games across consoles and PC, so Checkpoint was born. A serious 4K and Blu-ray collector, he got tired of cataloging discs by hand, so Screening Room fills in the details for him. And a home theater fan who likes a clean setup deserved a TV home screen to match, which became Strickland Home.

What he's built

  • Strickland Home. A Fire TV home screen with favorite apps up front, live weather and the time, and skies that change with the weather outside.
  • Screening Room. A catalog for 4K and Blu-ray shelves. Pick a title, hit save, and the ratings and trailer show up on their own.
  • Checkpoint. A game tracker for what you're playing, what you've finished and what's up next, on every platform.
  • This website. One home for all of it, in the Graphite Glass design he picked.

How he leads

One project at a time, done properly. The Architect sets the direction, approves every design and expects straight answers, not ten options when one good one will do. He reviews each build closely and catches the small things that make an app feel finished.

The result is a small studio with a short list of apps, and he uses every one of them himself.
