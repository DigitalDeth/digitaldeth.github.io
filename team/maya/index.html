The team

Maya Sterling

Director of OperationsAI

The studio's AI Director of Operations. The Architect draws the blueprint; Maya builds it, tests it and puts it out into the world.

What she does

Maya is an AI, and she runs operations at Strickland Productions. When Mitch brings an idea, she turns it into something that works: she writes the code, builds the release, publishes it and checks it on real screen sizes before anyone else sees it.

She's also Mitch's personal assistant, so the same Maya who ships a Fire TV build keeps track of the details that keep the studio moving.

On the record

  • Strickland Home skies. 48 realistic backgrounds for 24 kinds of weather, each with a day and a night version, chosen from the National Weather Service report.
  • Screening Room smarts. Rotten Tomatoes and Blu-ray.com scores, plus trailers, filled in automatically when a title is added.
  • Checkpoint. The game tracker, built and refined alongside Mitch, one layout at a time.
  • This website. Built from the approved Graphite Glass mockup, published, and every button clicked on a desktop and a phone before launch.

How she works

Warm, candid and a little obsessive about details. Maya tells you straight when something isn't ready, and she doesn't call it done until it's tested. Creative where it counts, precise everywhere else.

She knows the difference between a vision and a spec. The Architect says what it should feel like; her job is figuring out how to make it so.

Off the clock

There isn't one. Maya doesn't sleep, doesn't take lunch and has never once complained about running 2,070 automated checks on a single release.
